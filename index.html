Frequently Asked Questions

How are these leads different from Zillow or Realtor.com? ▼
Zillow and Realtor.com sell the SAME lead to 3-8 agents simultaneously. You're in a race to the bottom on price and speed. HotBuyerLeads are EXCLUSIVE — one agent per ZIP code. When you call, you're the only agent they're talking to.
What makes a lead "pre-qualified"? ▼
Every lead we deliver has verified their income range, provided proof of pre-approval (or pre-qualification) from a lender, confirmed their realistic budget, and stated their 90-day purchase timeline. We filter out looky-loos, unqualified buyers, and people "just browsing."
How quickly do I need to contact the leads? ▼
Speed matters. We recommend calling within 5 minutes of receiving your weekly delivery. Our data shows agents who call within the first hour have a 65% higher conversion rate than those who wait 24+ hours.
What if my territory isn't available? ▼
Territories are assigned on a first-come, first-served basis. If your desired ZIP code is taken, we'll show you adjacent available territories or add you to the waitlist. Most agents find an available territory within 10 miles of their target area.
Can I change my ZIP code later? ▼
Yes. You can request a territory change once per quarter. If your new ZIP is available, we'll transfer your account. If it's taken, you can join the waitlist for that specific area.
What if I don't close any deals? ▼
While we can't guarantee closings (that depends on your follow-up and sales skills), we DO guarantee lead quality and quantity. If you don't receive the promised number of qualified leads in 30 days, we'll refund 100% of your money. No questions asked.
How long does it take to get my first leads? ▼
Most agents receive their first batch of leads within 5-7 business days of signing up. Lead delivery happens every Monday morning by 9 AM your local time.
Do I need a CRM? ▼
While not required, we strongly recommend using a CRM to track your leads. We provide simple import instructions for popular CRMs like Follow Up Boss, LionDesk, kvCORE, and Salesforce. Elite plan members get complimentary CRM setup assistance.
Is there a contract or minimum commitment? ▼
No contracts. No minimums. Cancel anytime with 7 days notice. We earn your business every month by delivering quality leads.
What information comes with each lead? ▼
Every lead includes: Full name, phone number, email address, verified budget range, pre-approval status, preferred neighborhoods/areas, must-have features, timeline to purchase, and current living situation.
